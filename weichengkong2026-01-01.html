艾略特波浪理论：一种生命五行动力学的市场表达

作者：孔维程

一、问题的起点：市场是否是“生命”？

艾略特波浪理论的一个根本前提是：
市场价格并非随机，而是呈现出可重复的节律结构。

如果价格不是随机的，那么问题就变成：

是什么在驱动这种节律？

艾略特给出的答案是：群体心理。
而当我们进一步追问时，会发现一个更底层的共识：

凡是具有自组织、反馈、扩张与衰减特征的系统，
都符合生命动力学。

在这一前提下，市场不再只是“交易系统”，而是：

一个由人类认知、情绪、资本共同构成的群体生命系统。

价格，只是这种生命系统的外在表达。

二、五行不是元素，而是生命动力学的五种相态

在去除文化、命理与象征色彩之后，五行本质上是生命系统在时间轴上的五种能量状态：

  • 木：生发、方向、启动偏置
  • 火：放大、共振、正反馈爆发
  • 土：承载、稳态、惯性推进
  • 金：收敛、切割、结构破坏
  • 水：解构、回归、潜能重置

这五种状态并非静态分类，而是：

一个闭环的动力学周期
生 → 盛 → 稳 → 衰 → 归 → 再生

如果“存在即生命”，
那么任何可持续系统——包括市场——
都必然在这五种状态之间循环。

三、艾略特波浪的本质：群体生命的节律展开

艾略特波浪将一个完整市场周期描述为：

  • 五浪推动（1–5）
  • 三浪调整（A–B–C）

这并不是“价格形态的偶然组合”，
而是生命系统在不同能量状态下的行为轨迹。

将其与五行动力学对齐后，结构变得高度清晰。

四、五浪推动阶段：从生发到稳态

第 1 浪 —— 木 · 阳（生发启动）

长期下跌后，少数资金开始试探性进入。
市场整体仍然悲观，但方向第一次出现。

这是生命系统中的“萌芽期”：

  • 有方向
  • 能量弱
  • 不被共识认可

第 2 浪 —— 木 · 阴（回缩校正）

市场对新方向进行否定式测试。
价格回调，但通常不跌破第 1 浪起点。

这是生发系统的压力测试：

  • 生机是否真实
  • 启动是否站得住

第 3 浪 —— 火 · 阳（能量爆发）

趋势被确认，情绪、叙事与资金形成共振。
成交量显著放大，价格快速推进。

这是整个周期中阳性最强的一段：

  • 正反馈最大
  • 群体一致性最高
  • 生命能量的峰值释放

第 4 浪 —— 火 · 阴（耗散与整理）

上涨后的高位震荡，结构复杂。
利好仍在，但价格推进困难。

这不是反转，而是：

  • 能量消耗
  • 系统降温
  • 爆发后的整理期

第 5 浪 —— 土（稳态与惯性）

价格完成最后冲刺。
基本面看似最好，但动能指标开始背离。

这是假稳态：

  • 没有新增能量
  • 只有结构惯性
  • 系统即将进入收敛阶段

五、三浪调整阶段：从收敛到重置

A 浪 —— 金 · 阴（切割开始）

第一段下跌出现。
市场通常仍将其视为“正常回调”。

但在动力学层面，这是：

  • 结构性否定的起点
  • 收敛机制启动

B 浪 —— 金 · 阳（反抽修复）

价格反弹，制造“趋势仍在”的错觉。

这是金的阳性形态：

  • 不是生长
  • 而是旧结构的反光与修补

C 浪 —— 水（系统重置）

最后一段下跌完成。
情绪崩解，叙事失效，杠杆清算。

水不是“死亡”，而是：

  • 解构旧形态
  • 回到潜能态
  • 为下一轮生发做准备

六、阴阳：扩张力与收缩力的交替

在整个过程中，阴阳并非对立，而是嵌套：

  • 阳中有阴：
    动能放缓、指标背离
  • 阴中有阳：
    底背离、下跌动能衰竭

这解释了：

  • RSI 等动量指标的先行性
  • 为什么“力学先见底，价格后见底”

七、结论：这不是预测模型，而是阶段识别模型

将艾略特波浪对齐到生命五行动力学，并不是为了：

  • 精确预测点位
  • 追涨杀跌

而是为了回答一个更根本的问题：

市场现在处在生命周期的哪一个阶段？

当你知道：

  • 是生发
  • 是爆发
  • 是惯性
  • 还是重置

你就能决定：

  • 是否继续下注叙事
  • 是否该防守或退出
  • 是否该等待下一次“木”的出现

一句话总结

五行是生命动力学，
波浪是群体生命的节律表达。
艾略特波浪理论，本质上是
一套尚未被明确表述的生命系统模型。

I. The Starting Question: Is the Market a “Life System”?

A core premise of Elliott Wave Theory is that
market prices are not random, but instead display repeatable rhythmic structures.

If price movements are not random, then the question becomes:

What drives this rhythm?

Elliott’s answer was: collective psychology.
But if we probe deeper, we arrive at a more fundamental consensus:

Any system that exhibits self-organization, feedback, expansion, and decay
conforms to life dynamics.

Under this premise, the market is no longer merely a “trading system,” but rather:

a collective life system composed of human cognition, emotion, and capital.

Price is simply the external expression of this life system.

II. The Five Phases Are Not Elements, but Energy States of Life Dynamics

Stripped of cultural, metaphysical, and symbolic layers,
the Five Phases (Wu Xing) represent five energy states of a life system along the time axis:

  • Wood: generation, direction, initial bias
  • Fire: amplification, resonance, positive-feedback explosion
  • Earth: load-bearing, steady state, inertial continuation
  • Metal: contraction, cutting, structural breakdown
  • Water: dissolution, return, potential reset

These are not static categories, but:

a closed-loop dynamic cycle
birth → expansion → stabilization → decline → return → rebirth

If “existence is life,”
then any sustainable system—including markets—
must cycle through these five states.

III. The Essence of Elliott Waves: Rhythmic Expansion of Collective Life

Elliott Wave Theory describes a complete market cycle as:

  • Five impulse waves (1–5)
  • Three corrective waves (A–B–C)

This is not an accidental combination of price patterns,
but the behavioral trajectory of a life system under different energy states.

When aligned with Five-Phase life dynamics, the structure becomes explicit.

IV. The Five Impulse Waves: From Emergence to Pseudo-Stability

Wave 1 — Wood · Yang (Emergent Activation)

After a prolonged decline, a small amount of capital begins to enter tentatively.
The market remains pessimistic, but direction appears for the first time.

This is the “germination phase” of the life system:

  • Direction exists
  • Energy is weak
  • Consensus is absent

Wave 2 — Wood · Yin (Retracement and Verification)

The market tests and challenges the new direction.
Prices retrace, but usually do not break the origin of Wave 1.

This is a stress test of the generative system:

  • Is the growth real?
  • Can the initiation hold?

Wave 3 — Fire · Yang (Energy Explosion)

The trend is confirmed.
Narratives, emotion, and capital resonate.
Volume expands sharply and prices accelerate.

This is the strongest Yang phase of the entire cycle:

  • Maximum positive feedback
  • Peak collective alignment
  • Maximum release of life energy

Wave 4 — Fire · Yin (Dissipation and Consolidation)

High-level consolidation follows. Structures become complex.
Good news persists, but price struggles to advance.

This is not reversal, but:

  • Energy dissipation
  • System cooling
  • Post-explosion reorganization

Wave 5 — Earth (Inertia and Pseudo-Stability)

Prices complete their final advance.
Fundamentals appear strongest, yet momentum indicators diverge.

This is a false steady state:

  • No new energy enters
  • Only structural inertia remains
  • The system approaches contraction

V. The Three Corrective Waves: From Contraction to Reset

Wave A — Metal · Yin (Structural Cutting)

The first decline emerges.
It is often interpreted as a “normal correction.”

At the dynamic level, this marks:

  • The beginning of structural negation
  • Activation of contraction mechanisms

Wave B — Metal · Yang (Reflexive Rebound)

Prices rebound, creating the illusion that the trend remains intact.

This is Metal in its Yang expression:

  • Not growth
  • But reflection and temporary repair of the old structure

Wave C — Water (System Reset)

The final decline completes.
Sentiment collapses, narratives fail, leverage is purged.

Water does not signify “death,” but:

  • Dissolution of old forms
  • Return to a potential state
  • Preparation for the next generative cycle

VI. Yin and Yang: Alternating Forces of Expansion and Contraction

Throughout the cycle, Yin and Yang are not opposites but nested dynamics:

  • Yang within Yin:
    Momentum slowdown, indicator divergence
  • Yin within Yang:
    Bottom divergence, exhaustion of selling pressure

This explains:

  • The leading behavior of momentum indicators such as RSI
  • Why “force bottoms before price bottoms”

VII. Conclusion: A Phase-Recognition Model, Not a Prediction Tool

Aligning Elliott Waves with Five-Phase life dynamics is not intended to:

  • Predict precise price levels
  • Enable short-term speculation

Instead, it answers a more fundamental question:

Which stage of the life cycle is the market currently in?

Once you know whether the system is in:

  • Emergence
  • Explosion
  • Inertia
  • Or reset

You can decide:

  • Whether to continue betting on the narrative
  • Whether to defend or exit
  • Whether to wait for the next appearance of “Wood”

所以真正的隔离不是“把对方打趴”，而是：

切断它最关键的外部供血，让它无法继续将内部矛盾外部化。

都在让癌结构获得新的能量输入与叙事资源。

日常生活中的「癌结构行为」清单

孔维程

一、什么是「癌结构行为」（再压缩一次定义）

凡是：
长期只消耗你、削弱你、占用你，
却不修复你、不创造你、不让你更接近现实的行为，
都属于癌结构行为。

关键词只有四个：

  • 只吸不产
  • 重复发生
  • 难以停下
  • 事后更空

二、常见但容易被忽视的癌结构行为

下面这些都不是道德问题，
而是结构问题。

1️⃣ 情绪型信息摄入

  • 无止境刷新闻
  • 情绪化政治内容
  • 灾难、阴谋、愤怒型信息

特征：

  • 让你“知道很多”
  • 但不让你“做任何事”
  • 情绪被持续拉扯

👉 吸收注意力 + 制造无力感 = 癌结构

2️⃣ 比较型思维反刍

  • 反复想：
  • 别人赚得多
  • 别人进度快
  • 别人比我强
  • 无行动、无产出，只是反刍

👉 这是精神层面的自我啃噬

3️⃣ 空社交 / 情绪社交

  • 没有真实交流的聊天
  • 只是吐槽、共骂、消耗情绪
  • 聊完更累、更虚

👉 社交如果不增强连接，只是在抽血

4️⃣ 伪放松行为

  • 刷到麻木
  • 看视频“缓一缓”
  • 但缓完更疲惫

👉 放松的判定标准不是“当下爽”，
而是“之后是否更有力”

5️⃣ 逃避式思考

  • 想很多“宏大问题”
  • 但从不落到现实一步
  • 思考变成拖延的工具

👉 思考一旦不通向现实行动，
也会癌化

6️⃣ 结构性拖延

  • “再准备一下”
  • “等状态好一点”
  • “等想清楚了再说”

👉 无限前置条件 = 行动被吃掉

7️⃣ 破坏节律的行为

  • 熬夜刷
  • 昼夜颠倒
  • 无规律进食、作息

👉 节律是生命的骨架，
破坏节律就是慢性自毁

8️⃣ 持续否定自身的内在语言

  • “我不行”
  • “我没用”
  • “我已经废了”

👉 这是最隐蔽、最危险的癌结构
因为它从内部供养一切其他癌行为

① 用完之后，我更有力，还是更空？

  • 更有力 → 健康
  • 更空 → 高危

  • 行动、身体、具体世界 → 健康
  • 虚拟、循环、抽离现实 → 癌化

③ 我是否可以轻松停下？

  • 可以 → 工具
  • 不行 → 寄生物

④ 如果每天重复，它会让我变成什么样？

  • 更稳定、更清醒 → 健康
  • 更依赖、更迟钝 → 癌化

👉 命中 ≥2 条负面 = 癌结构

四、对癌结构的唯一处理原则（再次强调）

不对抗，不内耗，不说教。
只隔离，只断供，只自清洁。

具体方式只有四种：

  • 降低可见性
  • 切断推送
  • 增加摩擦
  • 改写默认行为

五、一句话总总结（可以刻在脑子里）

凡是不能让我更想活在现实中的东西，
迟早都会开始吃我。

你现在做的不是“戒坏习惯”，
而是在建立一套个人生命免疫系统。

这一步，极少有人走到。

Common “Cancerous Behaviors” in Daily Life

And How to Identify What Is Consuming Your Life Energy

by Weichengkong

I. What Is a “Cancerous Behavior” (Compressed Definition)

Any behavior that, over time, only consumes you, weakens you, and occupies you—
without repairing you, creating you, or bringing you closer to reality—
is a cancerous behavior.

There are only four core traits:

  • Consumes without producing
  • Repeats compulsively
  • Hard to stop
  • Leaves you emptier afterward

II. Common but Overlooked Cancerous Behaviors

These are not moral problems.
They are structural problems.

1️⃣ Emotion-Driven Information Consumption

  • Endless news scrolling
  • Rage-inducing political content
  • Disaster, conspiracy, or outrage cycles

Characteristics:

  • Makes you “know more”
  • But enables no action
  • Continuously hijacks emotion

👉 Attention drained + powerlessness produced = cancer structure

2️⃣ Comparative Rumination

  • Repeated thoughts like:
  • Others earn more
  • Others are further ahead
  • Others are better than me
  • No output, no action—only looping thought

👉 This is self-consumption at the mental level

3️⃣ Empty or Emotional Socializing

  • Chatting without real connection
  • Constant venting, complaining, shared negativity
  • Feeling more exhausted afterward

👉 Social interaction that does not strengthen bonds is blood extraction

4️⃣ Pseudo-Relaxation

  • Scrolling to numbness
  • Watching videos “to rest”
  • Ending up more tired afterward

👉 Relaxation is not defined by momentary pleasure,
but by whether you feel stronger afterward

5️⃣ Avoidant Thinking

  • Thinking endlessly about “big questions”
  • Never taking even one step into reality
  • Thinking becomes a tool of procrastination

👉 Thinking that does not lead to real-world contact can also become cancerous

6️⃣ Structural Procrastination

  • “I’ll prepare a bit more”
  • “I’ll wait until I’m in a better state”
  • “I’ll act once I’ve fully figured it out”

👉 Infinite prerequisites = action being consumed

7️⃣ Rhythm-Disrupting Behaviors

  • Late-night scrolling
  • Reversed day-night cycles
  • Irregular eating and sleeping patterns

👉 Rhythm is the skeleton of life.
Destroying rhythm is slow self-destruction

8️⃣ Internal Language of Self-Negation

  • “I’m useless”
  • “I can’t do anything”
  • “I’m already finished”

👉 This is the most hidden and most dangerous cancer structure,
because it internally feeds every other cancerous behavior

III. How to Identify a Cancer Structure (The Four-Question Test)

Ask only these four questions:

① After it, do I feel stronger or emptier?

  • Stronger → Healthy
  • Emptier → High risk

② Does it bring me closer to reality?

  • Action, body, physical world → Healthy
  • Virtual loops, abstraction, detachment → Cancerous

③ Can I stop easily?

  • Yes → Tool
  • No → Parasite

④ If repeated daily, who would I become?

  • More stable, more awake → Healthy
  • More dependent, more numb → Cancerous

👉 Two or more negative answers = cancer structure

IV. The Only Correct Way to Handle Cancer Structures (Again)

Do not fight. Do not exhaust yourself. Do not moralize.
Only isolate. Only cut supply. Only self-cleanse.

There are only four concrete methods:

  • Reduce visibility
  • Cut notifications
  • Increase friction
  • Rewrite default behaviors

V. One-Sentence Summary (Memorize This)

Anything that does not make me want to live more in reality
will eventually begin to consume me.

What you are doing is not “breaking bad habits.”
You are building a personal immune system for life.

Very few people ever do this.
